About Leonardo

Leonardo da Vinci was an Italian artist, inventor, scientist and engineer. He was born in 1452 in Vinci, Italy and died in 1519 in France. He is famous for his paintings and for his studies of science, anatomy and engineering.

Important Events

  1. 1452 - Leonardo was born in Vinci, Italy.
  2. He studied art in Florence.
  3. He worked in Milan as an artist and engineer.
  4. He painted the Mona Lisa.
  5. 1519 - Leonardo died in France.

His Contributions

  • Painting
  • Anatomical studies
  • Engineering designs
  • Scientific observations
  • Architecture
